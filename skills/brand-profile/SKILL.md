# Brand Profile Skill

## Purpose
Establishes authentic brand identity, target demographic persona, value propositions, and strict anti-slop guidelines across all generated content.

## Core Rules
1. **Zero AI Clichés**: Ban phrases like "in today's fast-paced digital world", "delve into", "testament to", "game changer", "tapestry", "revolutionize".
2. **Authentic Tone**: Ground every claim in concrete operational details, quantifiable benchmarks, or verifiable facts.
3. **Audience Alignment**: Speak directly to builder-level operators and creator-practitioners with high signal-to-noise ratio.
4. **Visual & Structural Consistency**: Standardize formatting, white-space cadence, and high-impact openers.
