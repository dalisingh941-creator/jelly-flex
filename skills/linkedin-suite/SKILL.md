# LinkedIn Suite

## Capabilities
- **"See More" Fold Optimization**:
  - The first 2 lines determine 90% of post impressions.
  - Line 1: Strong declarative hook.
  - Line 2: Tension gap forcing the click on "...see more".
- **Formatting**:
  - Clean single-sentence lines with whitespace.
  - Zero walls of text.
  - Conclude with a specific, opinionated discussion prompt.
- **PDF Document Carousels**:
  - 8-12 slides in 4:5 or 1:1 format.
  - High-contrast slide headers, minimal text per slide.
