# Content Calendar Skill

## Pacing Cadences
- **Instagram**: 1-2 Reels daily, 1 carousel every 2 days, 3-5 stories daily.
- **YouTube**: 1 Long-form weekly (Thursday/Sunday), 2 Shorts daily.
- **TikTok**: 2-3 short clips daily timed during peak morning/evening scroll hours.
- **X / Twitter**: 2 threads weekly, 3-5 high-signal single posts daily, 10 timeline replies.
- **LinkedIn**: 1 deep-dive post or PDF carousel daily (Tuesday–Thursday 8:00 AM EST).
