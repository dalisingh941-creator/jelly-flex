# Instagram Suite

## Capabilities
- **Reels Retention Scripting**:
  - 0-3s visual/verbal hook with on-screen text directive.
  - Retention beat every 4-5 seconds (b-roll switch, zoom, sound effect).
  - Looping final sentence transitioning back to the opening hook.
- **10-Slide Carousel Architecture**:
  - Slide 1: Bold title with curiosity gap (4:5 portrait 1080x1350).
  - Slides 2-4: Establish the problem and conventional mistakes.
  - Slides 5-8: Core value delivery, screenshots, architecture diagrams.
  - Slide 9: High-impact takeaway / summary cheat-sheet.
  - Slide 10: Clear CTA (Save for later / Comment keyword for resource).
- **Caption Keyword SEO**:
  - Primary keyword in first 5 words of caption.
  - Searchable paragraphs instead of hashtag walls.
  - Strategic 3-5 hyper-targeted niche tags at the bottom.
