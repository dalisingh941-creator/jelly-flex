# Hook Writer Skill

## Hook Archetypes
1. **Contrarian Truth**: "99% of creators do [X]. Here is why that guarantees failure."
2. **Quantified Case Study**: "How I reverse-engineered [X] in 48 hours without spending a dollar."
3. **Curiosity Gap**: "The one architecture trick that makes OpenClaw 10x faster (clean-room breakdown)."
4. **Actionable Checklist**: "Steal this 5-step clean-room reverse-engineering workflow."

## Guardrails
- First line MUST be under 120 characters to fit above the mobile fold.
- Zero introductory fluff ("Hey guys", "In this video", "Welcome back").
