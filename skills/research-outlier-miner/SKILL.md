# Research & Outlier Miner Suite

## Capabilities
- **Outlier Detection**:
  - Compares post engagement (likes, shares, comments) against the account's median baseline to identify 3x–10x breakout content.
- **Comment Mining**:
  - Scrapes audience questions and objections from top viral posts to identify unaddressed topics and pain points.
- **30-Day Viral Trend Synthesis**:
  - Tracks cross-platform narrative momentum across YouTube, TikTok, Reddit, and X to spot emerging concepts before saturation.
